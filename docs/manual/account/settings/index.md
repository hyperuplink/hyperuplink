# Settings

![The settings page](settings.webp)

Settings holds your language and your timezone. The language is the one the
board speaks to you in, on every page and in every message it sends you, and
it starts as the language your browser asked for when you signed up. The
timezone matters because the board stores every timestamp in UTC and renders
it in whatever you pick here. The dates on posts, the join dates on profiles
and the end date you gave a poll are all read in your own time rather than in
the server's.

A new account starts on UTC, which is correct for exactly the people who happen
to live on it, so this is worth setting once and then forgetting about.
